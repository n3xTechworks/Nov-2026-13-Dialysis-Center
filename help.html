Appointments and visits

How do I book my first visit?

Use the booking form on our Contact page or call +91 44 4960 2210. A coordinator will confirm a time within one working day. Visits are free.

Can I change or cancel a dialysis session?

Yes. Log in to the patient portal and open Schedule to reschedule, or call your centre. Please give at least 24 hours' notice so another patient can use the chair.

What time are dialysis shifts?

Day shifts run Monday to Saturday from 6:00 to 22:00 in three slots. Nocturnal dialysis runs overnight from 21:00 to 05:00 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday or Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.

Can a family member stay with me during treatment?

One companion is welcome in the treatment bay for the first few sessions and at any time in our family lounge. Overnight companions can rest in the lounge for nocturnal sessions.

Treatment and care

Which type of dialysis is right for me?

It depends on your health, your home, your work and what matters to you. Your nephrologist will explain in-centre, home, peritoneal and nocturnal dialysis, and you can try a session before deciding.

How long does a haemodialysis session take?

Most in-centre sessions last about four hours, three times a week. Nocturnal sessions are longer, around eight hours, which is gentler on the heart.

Does dialysis hurt?

Needle insertion into a fistula can sting briefly; numbing cream helps. Most people rest, read or sleep during treatment. Tell your nurse at once about cramps, dizziness or nausea.

What if I feel unwell between sessions?

Call your centre during opening hours or the 24/7 on-call line. For chest pain, severe breathlessness or heavy bleeding, call 108 first.

Billing and insurance

How much does dialysis cost?

In-centre haemodialysis starts from ₹2,600 per session. Your written plan lists every cost before you start, with no hidden charges for consumables or nursing.

Do you accept insurance and government schemes?

Yes. We work with most major insurers, CGHS and state dialysis schemes. Our coordinators handle pre-authorisation and cashless claims where possible.

How do I pay my bills?

Pay by UPI, card or net banking in the patient portal under Billing, or at reception. You will receive a receipt by email straight away.

Can I get an itemised invoice for my insurer?

Yes. Every invoice in the portal can be downloaded as a PDF with session dates, codes and a breakdown of charges.

Patient portal and account

How do I get a patient portal account?

Once you are registered as a Tidewell patient, choose Sign up on the login page and use the email you gave us. You can also sign in with Google or Apple.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Choose Forgot password on the login page and we will email you a reset link. The link works for 30 minutes.

What can I see in the portal?

Your upcoming sessions, past session details such as weight, blood pressure and Kt/V, lab results, prescriptions, care plans, invoices and payment methods.

Can my carer use the portal for me?

Yes. You can add a carer from your profile. They get their own login and can see the sections you choose.
