Your health information is personal. This policy explains, in plain language, what Tidewell Dialysis Centre does with it. It applies to our centres, this website and the patient portal. This is a sample policy for a demonstration website.

1. Who we are

Tidewell Dialysis Centre, 14 Marina Crescent, Besant Nagar, Chennai 600090, is responsible for your information. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the medical record rules that apply to clinics in India.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email and address, and those of your emergency contact.
  • Health information: diagnoses, dialysis prescriptions, session records, lab results, medicines and care plans.
  • Billing details: insurance or scheme numbers, invoices and payment references. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Website and portal use: pages visited, device type and sign-in times, used to keep the service secure.

3. How we use it

We use your information to provide safe dialysis care, schedule your sessions, share results with you, process payments and insurance claims, and send reminders you have agreed to. We use anonymised data to improve the quality of our care. We never sell your information.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what is needed, with: doctors and hospitals involved in your care, your insurer or government scheme when you ask us to claim, laboratories that process your samples, and trusted IT providers bound by confidentiality contracts. We may share information when the law requires it or to protect someone's life.

5. How long we keep it

Medical records are kept for at least seven years after your last treatment, or longer when the law requires. Website enquiries that do not lead to treatment are deleted after 12 months.

6. Your rights

  • See and receive a copy of your information.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Withdraw consent for reminders or marketing at any time.
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer need to keep.
  • Nominate someone to act for you, and raise a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

Records are encrypted in storage and in transit. Staff access is limited to what their role needs and is logged. The portal uses secure sign-in and signs you out after a period of inactivity.

8. Cookies

This website uses only essential storage to remember your theme, text direction and portal session. We do not use advertising cookies.

9. Contact us

Write to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@tidewell.example or call +91 44 4960 2210. You can also read our Terms of Service.
