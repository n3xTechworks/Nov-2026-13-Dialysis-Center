How we work

One plan, shared by everyone who cares for you

Your team meets every month to review your results, your goals and how you are feeling.

A named nephrologist

The same consultant reviews you at least monthly and adjusts your prescription as your needs change.

Monthly review

A primary nurse

Your primary nurse knows your access, your routine and the little things that make sessions easier.

1 nurse for every 4 patients

Specialist support

Dietitians, an access surgeon, a counsellor and a coordinator join in whenever you need them.

Included in your care

Help at any hour

A nephrologist is on call around the clock, and home patients have a 24/7 nurse helpline.

24/7 on call
