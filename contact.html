Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about visits, referrals and costs. There is more in our help centre.

Do I need a referral to book a visit?

No. Anyone can book a free visit. If you decide to start treatment with us, we will ask for a referral letter and recent results from your current kidney doctor, and we can request them for you.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring your latest blood tests, a list of your medicines, any discharge summaries and your insurance or scheme card. A family member or friend is very welcome to come with you.

How quickly can I start dialysis with you?

For people already on dialysis elsewhere, we can usually offer a regular slot within one to two weeks once your viral screening results are in. Urgent starts are discussed with the on-call nephrologist the same day.

Do you accept insurance and government schemes?

Yes. We work with most major insurers, CGHS and state dialysis schemes, including the Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme where eligible. Our coordinators help with pre-authorisation and paperwork.

Can I arrange holiday dialysis at Tidewell?

Yes. Visiting patients are welcome at all three centres. Please contact us at least four weeks before you travel so we can collect your reports and hold a slot.

Is there transport to and from sessions?

We run a shared pick-up service within 8 km of each centre and can help you arrange a wheelchair-accessible taxi. Ask your coordinator to add you to the transport list.
