Using this website

This website is run by Tidewell Dialysis Centre. By using it you agree to these terms. This is a sample document for a demonstration website.

  • Content is for general information and does not replace advice from your own doctor.
  • In an emergency, call 108. Do not use website forms for urgent medical needs.
  • You may share our articles for personal, non-commercial use with a link back to the page.
  • We work to keep information accurate but may update services, prices and hours without notice.

Links to other websites

We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of websites we link to, such as maps or government scheme portals.

Your patient portal account

  • Accounts are for registered Tidewell patients and the carers they invite.
  • Keep your password private and sign out on shared devices. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used your account.
  • Results appear in the portal after a clinician has reviewed them. Contact your care team if anything is unclear.
  • We may suspend an account that is misused or put other patients' information at risk.

Availability

We aim to keep the portal available at all times, but planned maintenance may take place overnight on Sundays. Your centre can always help by phone.

Visits and dialysis sessions

  • A visit request is confirmed only when a coordinator calls or emails you.
  • Please give at least 24 hours' notice to change or cancel a session, so the chair can go to another patient.
  • Please arrive 15 minutes before your session for weight and blood pressure checks.
  • Holiday dialysis requires recent screening results before your first session.

Our commitment

If we need to move your session, we will tell you as early as possible and offer the nearest available slot.

Fees and payment

  • Prices are in Indian rupees and include GST where it applies. Your written care plan lists every cost before treatment.
  • Invoices are due within 15 days unless covered by an approved insurance or scheme claim.
  • If an insurer declines a claim, we will explain why and agree a plan with you before charging.
  • Refunds for prepaid sessions you could not attend are made to the original payment method within 7 working days.

Questions

Contact our billing team at billing@tidewell.example or read our Privacy Policy.
